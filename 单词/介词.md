
# 一、按功能分类整理

## 1. 时间介词

| 介词 | 音标 | 核心意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| at | /æt/ | 在（时间点） | Meet me at noon. |
| on | /ɒn/ | 在（具体某天） | We met on Monday. |
| in | /ɪn/ | 在（月份、季节、年份、较长时期） | in May, in 2024 |
| before | /bɪˈfɔː(r)/ | 在……之前 | Wash your hands before dinner. |
| after | /ˈɑːftə(r)/ | 在……之后 | We met after class. |
| during | /ˈdjʊərɪŋ/ | 在……期间 | He slept during the movie. |
| since | /sɪns/ | 自……以来 | We’ve known each other since 2018. |
| until / till | /ənˈtɪl/ /tɪl/ | 直到……为止 | Stay until I return. / Wait till midnight. |
| by | /baɪ/ | 在……之前（不迟于） | Finish it by Friday. |
| through | /θruː/ | 贯穿；自始至终 | Rain throughout the night. |
| throughout | /θruːˈaʊt/ | 遍及；在整个……期间 | He worked throughout the year. |
| within | /wɪˈðɪn/ | 在……之内 | Finish within an hour. |
| following | /ˈfɒləʊɪŋ/ | 在……之后 | Following lunch, we met. |
| prior to | /ˈpraɪə(r) tu/ | 在……之前（正式） | Prior to leaving, call me. |
| circa | /ˈsɜːkə/ | 大约（年代） | Built circa 1900. |

**易混：**
- **at** 用于时间点（at 6, at noon）；**on** 用于具体某天（on Monday, on May 1st）；**in** 用于月份、季节、年份、世纪（in May, in summer, in 2024）。
- **since** 后接时间点，常与现在完成时连用；**for** 后接时间段，如 `for two years`。
- **until/till** 表示持续到某时；**by** 表示不迟于某时。

## 2. 地点、位置、方向介词

| 介词 | 音标 | 核心意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| at | /æt/ | 在（某点） | at the bus stop |
| in | /ɪn/ | 在……里面 | The cat is in the box. |
| on | /ɒn/ | 在……上面 | The book is on the desk. |
| above | /əˈbʌv/ | 在……上方（不接触） | The lamp hung above the table. |
| over | /ˈəʊvə(r)/ | 在……上方；越过 | The plane flew over the city. |
| below | /bɪˈləʊ/ | 在……下方 | The valley lies below the ridge. |
| under | /ˈʌndə(r)/ | 在……下面 | Shoes under the bed. |
| underneath | /ˌʌndəˈniːθ/ | 在……下面（强调覆盖） | A letter underneath the book. |
| beneath | /bɪˈniːθ/ | 在……下面（正下方，正式） | The note was beneath the mat. |
| beside | /bɪˈsaɪd/ | 在……旁边 | Sit beside me. |
| alongside | /əˌlɒŋˈsaɪd/ | 在……旁边；沿着 | The boat moved alongside the pier. |
| near | /nɪə(r)/ | 在……附近 | A park near the station. |
| by | /baɪ/ | 在旁边 | A house by the sea. |
| between | /bɪˈtwiːn/ | 在……之间（两者） | The café sits between two banks. |
| among | /əˈmʌŋ/ | 在……之间（三者或以上） | She shared snacks among friends. |
| amongst | /əˈmʌŋst/ | 同 among（英式） | Rumors spread amongst the team. |
| around | /əˈraʊnd/ | 围绕；大约 | Flowers grew around the tree. |
| round | /raʊnd/ | 围绕（英式） | We sat round the table. |
| through | /θruː/ | 穿过 | They walked through the tunnel. |
| across | /əˈkrɒs/ | 穿过（从一边到另一边） | They ran across the field. |
| along | /əˈlɒŋ/ | 沿着 | We strolled along the river. |
| past | /pɑːst/ | 经过；超过 | We drove past the farm. |
| beyond | /bɪˈjɒnd/ | 在……那边；超出 | The lake lies beyond the forest. |
| opposite | /ˈɒpəzɪt/ | 在……对面 | The library opposite the church. |
| inside | /ˌɪnˈsaɪd/ | 在……内部 | The toys are inside the chest. |
| outside | /ˌaʊtˈsaɪd/ | 在……外面 | Let’s sit outside the house. |
| into | /ˈɪntə/ | 进入……里面 | She jumped into the pool. |
| onto | /ˈɒntə/ | 到……上面 | The cat leapt onto the counter. |
| out | /aʊt/ | 从……里面出去 | She ran out the door. |
| up | /ʌp/ | 向上；沿着……往上 | Climb up the stairs. |
| down | /daʊn/ | 沿着……往下 | The ball rolled down the hill. |
| off | /ɒf/ | 从……离开 | He fell off the ladder. |
| toward | /təˈwɔːd/ | 朝；向 | He walked toward the exit. |
| towards | /təˈwɔːdz/ | 同 toward（英式） | She cycled towards town. |
| to | /tuː/ | 到；向；给 | Give it to me. |
| upon | /əˈpɒn/ | 在……之上（正式） | Upon arrival, check in. |
| atop | /əˈtɒp/ | 在……顶端 | A flag waved atop the tower. |
| astride | /əˈstraɪd/ | 跨坐 | He sat astride the horse. |
| aboard | /əˈbɔːd/ | 登上（交通工具） | They went aboard the ship. |
| amid | /əˈmɪd/ | 在……之中 | The cabin stood amid the trees. |
| amidst | /əˈmɪdst/ | 同 amid | Peace amidst the chaos. |

**易混：**
- **above / over**：above 不接触，over 可表示覆盖、越过。
- **below / under / beneath / underneath**：below 下方，under 正下方或覆盖，beneath 正式，underneath 强调覆盖。
- **between / among**：between 两者，among 三者及以上。
- **beside / besides**：beside 在旁边，besides 除……之外还有。
- **in / into**：in 静态，into 动态进入。
- **on / onto**：on 静态，onto 动态到上面。
- **through / across**：through 从内部穿过，across 从表面横过。

## 3. 方式、手段、工具介词

| 介词 | 音标 | 核心意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| by | /baɪ/ | 通过；乘坐；被 | by bus, by email |
| with | /wɪð/ | 用；和……一起；带有 | She came with friends. |
| without | /wɪˈðaʊt/ | 没有 | Don’t go without a coat. |
| via | /ˈvaɪə/ | 通过；经由 | Travel via Delhi. |
| per | /pɜː(r)/ | 每；按照 | $20 per hour. |
| as | /æz/ | 作为 | He served as treasurer. |
| like | /laɪk/ | 像……一样 | He looks like his father. |
| unlike | /ˌʌnˈlaɪk/ | 不像 | Unlike her brother, she’s quiet. |

**易混：**
- **by / with**：by 表示手段或交通工具，with 表示工具或伴随。
- **as / like**：as 表示“作为”，like 表示“像”。

## 4. 原因、目的、关于介词

| 介词 | 音标 | 核心意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| for | /fɔː(r)/ | 为了；给；因为 | A present for you. |
| because of | /bɪˈkɒz əv/ | 因为 | because of the rain |
| due to | /djuː tu/ | 由于 | due to bad weather |
| owing to | /ˈəʊɪŋ tu/ | 由于 | owing to the strike |
| thanks to | /θæŋks tu/ | 多亏 | thanks to your help |
| about | /əˈbaʊt/ | 关于 | She worried about the exam. |
| concerning | /kənˈsɜːnɪŋ/ | 关于 | A memo concerning the policy. |
| regarding | /rɪˈɡɑːdɪŋ/ | 关于 | Questions regarding the bill. |
| re | /riː/ | 关于（邮件） | An email re scheduling. |
| of | /ɒv/ | ……的；属于 | A cup of tea. |
| on | /ɒn/ | 关于 | a book on history |

**易混：**
- **about / on**：about 泛指，on 用于学术、正式“关于”。
- **concerning / regarding / re**：都表示“关于”，concerning/regarding 较正式，re 用于邮件标题。
- **for / because of / due to**：for 后接名词，because of 后接名词，due to 后接名词，但 due to 常作表语。

## 5. 让步、条件、排除介词

| 介词 | 音标 | 核心意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| despite | /dɪˈspaɪt/ | 尽管 | We played despite the rain. |
| notwithstanding | /ˌnɒtwɪθˈstændɪŋ/ | 尽管 | Notwithstanding objections, it passed. |
| in spite of | /ɪn spaɪt əv/ | 尽管 | in spite of the rain |
| except | /ɪkˈsept/ | 除了……之外 | Everyone left except me. |
| excepting | /ɪkˈseptɪŋ/ | 同 except | Excepting holidays, we’re open daily. |
| excluding | /ɪkˈskluːdɪŋ/ | 不包括 | Tickets excluding fees cost less. |
| barring | /ˈbɑːrɪŋ/ | 除非；除……外 | Barring accidents, we’ll arrive on time. |
| save | /seɪv/ | 除了 | Save one error, it’s perfect. |
| failing | /ˈfeɪlɪŋ/ | 如果没有 | Failing payment, access ends. |
| given | /ˈɡɪvn/ | 考虑到 | Given the time, we’ll stop here. |
| considering | /kənˈsɪdərɪŋ/ | 考虑到 | Considering the weather, we left early. |
| including | /ɪnˈkluːdɪŋ/ | 包括 | The price, including tax, is fair. |
| plus | /plʌs/ | 加 | Three plus four is seven. |
| minus | /ˈmaɪnəs/ | 减去 | Ten minus three is seven. |
| times | /taɪmz/ | 乘以（非介词） | Three times four is twelve. |
| versus | /ˈvɜːsəs/ | 对抗；与……相对 | The case versus Smith. |

**易混：**
- **except / besides**：except 排除，besides 包括。
- **except / excepting / save / barring**：都表示排除，except 最常用，save 古旧，barring 表示“除非”。
- **despite / in spite of / notwithstanding**：都表示让步，despite 后接名词，in spite of 后接名词，notwithstanding 较正式。
- **given / considering**：都表示“考虑到”，后接名词或从句。

# 二、高频易混介词对比

| 对比 | 区别 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| in / on / at（时间） | in 年/月/季节，on 具体某天，at 时间点 | in May, on Monday, at 6 |
| in / on / at（地点） | in 大地点/内部，on 表面，at 具体点 | in China, on the table, at the door |
| above / over | above 不接触，over 覆盖/越过 | above the clouds, over the bridge |
| below / under / beneath / underneath | below 下方，under 正下方/覆盖，beneath 正式，underneath 强调覆盖 | below sea level, under the bed, beneath the stars, underneath the rug |
| between / among | between 两者，among 三者及以上 | between you and me, among friends |
| beside / besides | beside 在旁边，besides 除……之外还有 | sit beside me, besides math |
| except / besides | except 排除，besides 包括 | Everyone except Tom. / Besides Tom, everyone came. |
| by / with | by 手段/交通工具，with 工具/伴随 | by car, with a pen |
| for / since | for 时间段，since 时间点 | for two years, since 2020 |
| until / by | until 持续到，by 不迟于 | wait until 6, finish by 6 |
| during / for | during 在……期间，for 持续多久 | during the movie, for two hours |
| through / across | through 内部穿过，across 表面横过 | through the tunnel, across the street |
| into / in | into 动态进入，in 静态在内 | jump into the pool, swim in the pool |
| onto / on | onto 动态到上面，on 静态在上面 | jump onto the table, sit on the table |
| toward / to | toward 朝方向，to 到达 | walk toward the door, go to the door |
| like / as | like 像，as 作为 | He looks like his father. / He works as a teacher. |
| despite / although | despite 介词+名词，although 连词+从句 | despite the rain, although it rained |
| because of / because | because of 介词+名词，because 连词+从句 | because of the rain, because it rained |

# 三、学习建议

1. **分类记忆**：按时间、地点、方向、方式、原因、让步、排除等分类，比按字母背更有效。
2. **搭配记忆**：介词常与动词、形容词、名词固定搭配，如 `depend on`, `interested in`, `afraid of`。
3. **对比记忆**：把易混介词成对记，如 `between/among`, `beside/besides`, `except/besides`。
4. **例句记忆**：每个介词记 1-2 个典型例句，比背中文释义更牢。
5. **注意英式/美式差异**：amongst/among, towards/toward, round/around 等。
6. **写作中主动使用**：读完介词后，自己造句，尤其是易混介词。
7. **不要死记 85 个**：先掌握最高频的 20-30 个（at, in, on, by, with, for, from, to, of, about, after, before, during, under, over, between, among, through, without, against 等），再扩展。
